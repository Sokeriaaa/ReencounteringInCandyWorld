# 🍭 Reencountering in Candy World

**English** | [中文](README.zh-cn.md)

A continuation of Candy World by [ochotonida](https://github.com/ochotonida)
([1.12](https://github.com/ochotonida/candymod)) and [Mrbysco](https://github.com/Mrbysco)
([1.16 & 1.17](https://github.com/Mrbysco/CandyWorld)) for modern Minecraft versions.
It brings back the iconic candy-filled biomes, delightful mobs, and delicious tools,
all about that sweet taste of nostalgia and discovery.
Embark on a journey to rediscover a world made of confectionery wonders!

## 🌌 The Candy Dimension (WIP)

Venture into a dedicated dimension overflowing with sweets!
While many features can be found rarely in the Overworld, the Candy World is where the true adventure begins.

* **Teleporter**: Craft and eat a Magic Piece of Candy to teleport to this sugary realm.
  Don't worry, the item isn't consumed upon use, ensuring you always have a way back home.
  (Pro Tip: Use JEI for recipes!)
* **Renewable**: More Magic Candy can be found generating underground within the dimension itself.

## ⚒️ Edible Tools & Crafting

Why just use tools when you can eat them too? Two new material types: **Chocolate** and **Cotton Candy**.

* **Edible Tools**: Simply **sneak and right-click** while holding any tool to take a bite!
  The amount of hunger restored depends on the tool's material and its remaining durability.
* **New Craft Tables**: For your convenience, custom crafting tables generate within the dimension,
  so you can craft on the go without bringing your own.

## 🍬 Biomes

Discover three incredibly sweet, but rare, biomes in the Overworld.
For a guaranteed encounter, journey to the Candy World dimension!
Now we are using [TerraBlender](https://github.com/Glitchfiend/TerraBlender) to bring these delicious biomes to the
Overworld!

### Cotton Candy Plains

A fluffy landscape where cotton candy grows like grass.

* **Blocks**: The ground is made of **Brownies**, and the trees have trunks of **Candy Cane**.
  A pickaxe is recommended for harvesting these sweet trees.
* **Resources**: An abundant source of **Cotton Candy**.

### Chocolate Forest

A rich and decadent forest where chocolate flows like water.

* **Blocks**: Find **Milk, White, and Dark Chocolate** blocks alongside **Wafer Sticks**.
* **Ores**: **Cookie Ore** generates in abundance here, and can also be found in the other biomes.

### Gummy Swamp

The most vibrant and colorful biome, with a terrain made of five different types of chewy **Gummy** blocks.

* **Blocks**: Look for giant **Gummy Worms** that commonly spawn here.

## 🐾 Mobs

Meet the adorable (and edible?) inhabitants of the sweet biomes.

### Candy Cotton Sheep

Found grazing in the **Cotton Candy Plains**.

* **Behavior**: Looks like a regular sheep, but its wool is made of **Cotton Candy**!
* **Harvest**: Use a stick to sheer them. They regrow their fluff by eating Cotton Candy Grass or when fed bone meal.
* **Breeding**: Breed them with **Sugar**.

### Easter Chicken

Native to the **Chocolate Forest**.

* **Behavior**: Lays three types of chocolate eggs: **Milk, White, and Dark**!
* **Acceleration**: Feeding them chocolate will make them lay eggs faster.
* **Breeding**: Breed them with **Wafer Sticks**.

### Gummy Mice & Gummy Bears

Inhabitants of the **Gummy Swamp**.

* **Mice**: Skittish little creatures that will flee if players get too close!
* **Bears**: Rarer than the mice, and come in the five classic gummy colors found in the swamp.
* **Behavior**: Both add life and color to the vibrant swamp.
